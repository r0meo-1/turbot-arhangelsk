# TurBot — Viral AI Avatar Campaign

## Goal
Use the new TurBot travel avatar as a high-energy social creative that drives users into the 3-question tour-selection flow.

## Core message
**«Всего 3 вопроса — и мы подберём ваш идеальный тур»**

## CTA
**Подобрать тур за 3 вопроса**

## Landing
https://r0meo-1.github.io/turbot-arhangelsk/turbot/

## Channels
- VK Clips / VK Stories
- Instagram Reels / Stories
- Telegram posts
- Landing hero / campaign block

## Creative direction
- Friendly female travel creator + TurBot robot mascot
- Tropical beach, turquoise water, bright daylight
- Strong blue/white TurBot branding with orange CTA accent
- Three UI prompts: destination, travel date, budget
- Telegram and VK cues
- Mobile-first composition with readable Russian copy
